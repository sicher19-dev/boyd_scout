Five keys

  1. Changeup below the knees. To righties, your changeup under 1.5 ft gets a 46% whiff rate (.291 xwOBA). It's been the pitch of your September: 38% usage to righties over the last four starts, .261 xwOBA. The ones that finish below the knees are where the swing-and-miss lives.
  2. Heater at the letters or above. At the letters your four-seam has been worth +3.9 runs this year, and above the zone it gets 35% whiffs. Tatis and Machado have flat swings that cover the letters, so for those two, finishing it above the zone is the play.
  3. Curveball for strike one. 36% CSW, the best of your four pitches, and you already open righties with it 24% of the time. Hays (.001 xwOBA vs lefty curves) and France (.115) are the two to use it on.
  4. Hitter's counts, go soft. Tatis (.521) and France (.558) are at their best when they're ahead. When you're behind to the middle of the order, the changeup below the knees is the pitch.
  5. Take the free strikes. Campusano (73%), Bogaerts (67%) and Cronenworth (62%) take first-pitch strikes. Taylor and Hays swing early, so they get the curve or an edge.
