Five things for tonight

  1. Changeup below the knees or not at all. Against righties, his changeup below 1.5 ft draws a 46% whiff rate (.291 xwOBA). At zone height it is 28% (.305), and at mid-thigh or higher it is 8% (.345). In September only 22% of his changeups to righties finished below the knees, down from 32% on the season. That share has to come back tonight.
  2. No belt-high fastballs. His four-seam has lost ride since spring (13–14" in March/April, 10–11" now). At belt height it is his worst pitch (−4.1 runs). At the letters it is worth +3.9 runs. Against Tatis and Machado, who have flat bats, the letters are not enough. The fastball has to finish above the zone.
  3. Curveball for strike one. 36% CSW, the best of his four pitches. He already opens righties with it 24% of the time. Hays (.001 xwOBA vs LHP curves) and France (.115) are the names.
  4. Hitters' counts get no zone fastballs to the red boxes. Tatis .521 and France .558 xwOBA when ahead in the count. Machado's 6/30 homer came 2-1. Behind in the count, the changeup below the knees is the pitch.
  5. Take the free strikes. Campusano (73%), Bogaerts (67%), and Cronenworth (62%) take first-pitch strikes. Samad Taylor and Hays swing early, so they get the curve or an edge.

Game plan

Mix vs this lineup

  • Four-seam ~40%, at the letters or above. Nothing at the belt.
  • Changeup ~35% to righties, finishing below the knees. It is his best pitch against this lineup.
  • Curveball ~15%, mostly for strike one and as a steal pitch to France and Hays.
  • Slider ~10%, aimed at Merrill and Cronenworth, away. Keep it off Machado's inner half.

Times through the order

  • Expect a short leash: the likely plan is twice through, around 18 batters.
  • His 2026 xwOBA barely moves by pass (.327 / .339 / .325), so there is no cliff to fear on a second look. The concern is Tatis and Machado seeing him a second time.
  • Second time through: flip the first-pitch pattern. Changeup below the knees for strike one to Tatis and Machado instead of the curve.

First inning

  • Tatis: curve or changeup below the knees. No first-pitch fastball in the zone. He homered to lead off the 6/30 game.
  • Taylor: attack with the fastball at the letters. Softest bat on the roster.
  • Machado: fastball up, changeup down, nothing at the belt. Don't let him hit with a runner on in a hitter's count.

Leverage spots

  • Runner on, Machado or France up, count 2-0 / 2-1 / 3-1: changeup below the knees. A walk is better than a middle fastball.
  • Merrill with two strikes: fastball above the zone (67% whiff vs LHP) or a slider away.
  • Bottom third (Bogaerts, Cronenworth, Campusano): take the free strike one, then expand. These are the outs to get quickly.

Gamebar

Fastball at the letters or above. Changeup below the knees. Curveball for strike one. In a hitter's count, Tatis, Machado, and France get nothing in the zone.
